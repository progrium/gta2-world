GTA2 World

An open-source remake of GTA2 that runs in your web browser, with online multiplayer. Set up in three steps, then play.

1

Get the game files

The game runs on the data files from a copy of GTA2. Rockstar gave GTA2 away as freeware, so you can download it straight from the Internet Archive in the page, or pick the folder of a copy you already have. The files are checked and kept in this browser only.

Play with others

Host a game in your browser tab and send the invite link to your friends, or open a link someone sent you.

Or play on your own

Drive any of the maps offline: walk, jump, steal cars and try the weapons. Keyboard, gamepad or the touch pad on phones and tablets.

Help out

Small jobs for anyone who wants to help the project. Each takes a few minutes and ends with a file you send on GitHub.

  • Match sounds to eventsListen to the game's sound banks and pick the right sample for each gunshot, crash and engine. Needs your game files.
  • Rate the car handlingFor people who know GTA2: drive the rebuilt handling and say how close it feels. Needs your game files.
  • Check your game controllerA 3 minute check of buttons, sticks, delay and rumble on an Xbox, DualSense or Switch Pro pad.
  • Check your browserPress one button to check that the simulation gives the same result in your browser. 2 minutes.
