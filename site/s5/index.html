How to run the rating

  1. Load your nyc.gci and the matching .sty, pick a car you also have footage of, and open the footage on the right.
  2. Each rater drives for about five minutes: a straight run to top speed, the block circuits, the open lot for handbrake turns, and a few hits on walls.
  3. Raters fill in the sheet alone, without seeing the others' answers first.
  4. The spike passes when three people who know the game each answer yes to "Recognisably GTA2".
