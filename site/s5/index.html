Handling bench

The game's own car handling, rebuilt from written behaviour notes and run in WebAssembly at the original's 30 ticks per second on the synthetic test city. Drive it next to footage of the original and rate whether it feels like GTA2.

Your data files

The handling constants are read from your own copy of the game. Files stay in this browser; nothing is uploaded. Until you load them, the car runs on our own placeholder values, which are not the original's. The .sty gives each car its size and wheel positions; without it every car is a 32 by 64 pixel saloon.

or drop them here. Both live in the game's data folder.

How to run the rating

  1. Load your nyc.gci and the matching .sty, pick a car you also have footage of, and open the footage on the right.
  2. Each rater drives for about five minutes: a straight run to top speed along the top road, laps of the city blocks, handbrake turns on the open fields west of the city (turn round from the start), and a few hits on building walls.
  3. Raters fill in the sheet alone, without seeing the others' answers first.
  4. The spike passes when three people who know the game each answer yes to "Recognisably GTA2".
