S6 Gamepad Probe

1. Environment and pads

Live state

Press any button on a controller. Browsers hide pads until a button is pressed.

2. Mapping check

Prompts you for each standard-mapping control by position. Press and release each. Skip anything the OS captures (Guide/PS/Home often open a system overlay).

Not running.

3. Axis ranges and triggers

8 seconds: roll both sticks around their outer edge several times, and squeeze both triggers slowly from zero to full and back. Then let go of everything; the last 2 seconds measure rest drift.

Not running.

4. Polling latency

10 seconds: wiggle the left stick continuously and tap face buttons. Keep this tab focused and the mouse still. Measures how often the browser's pad state changes, how stale it is when read in requestAnimationFrame, and how many updates a once-per-frame read misses.

Not running.

5. Rumble

Fire each effect, then mark whether you felt it.

effectresultfelt?

6. Hot-plug

With the page focused: unplug (or power off) the pad, wait 3 s, reconnect, press a button. Then repeat while this tab is in the background, and come back. Optionally connect a second pad.
