| 3.4 Following the road | ours | sim/test/traffic_rules.zig | Lane arrows, straight-on else a once-per-junction 50/50 pick, junction right-of-way sweep (50-tick yield), settle into the lane with the gap check, seek road (ahead, left, right, behind; one level down). Ours: the sweep box (2.5 ahead, 4 x 8 blocks), seek road turns toward the road rather than reversing. Lane centring holds its line while the gap toward the lane centre is taken (the note's settle gap check, applied to the continuous nudge too); seek road looks up to 3 blocks out (the note says 1); a junction cell without arrows lets traffic go straight. Task cars use the route planner (A* over road cells, junctions cost one more) and steer at the route's block two moves on, slowing to 0.1 for a turn, instead of the note's pending-turn shortcut. Ours: the junction pick is made one or two blocks before the junction and a turning car steers at its exit lane from the approach on (the AI's turning circle is about 1.5 blocks, so turning only inside the junction swung cars into the far lane); routes never reverse and keep two straight blocks between turns within 8 route steps of the car, so a task car never plans a U-turn inside a junction. Ours, from the real-map traffic soak (`tools/level --traffic`): a car starts its turn at a bend in its lane (a lane cell ahead whose arrow is at right angles) up to 2 blocks early, slows to 0.05 for a bend or a turn, and a car that ran wide onto a lane going the other way steers back to the lane beside it that runs its way instead of turning round. |
| 3.5 Looking ahead | ours | sim/test/traffic_rules.zig | The car's own box at 1.75 (1 and 8 degrees into a turn), then 1 block; 20-tick grace, never for buses (`ai_bus`), not on ramps or backing out; brake (handbrake above 0.4) or, within a block at a steep angle or the player's car, honk and coast; honk 1 car in 8, emergency (`ai_emergency`) always; crossing-queue overtake near a marker. Ours: the face-off case backs out after 10 ticks, a car stopped behind a stopped car in its own lane after 150; the wiggle waits 5 ticks and uses a gentle throttle; a wall backs out for 25 ticks after 10 at a standstill; the overtake's swing; honking at a ped the car is waiting on. The note's 1-in-4 horn flash is not separate from the honk. |

| 3.7 Chase, follow and patrol tasks | ours | sim/test/traffic_rules.zig | `car_tasks` API: chase, follow, patrol, go to, park (go to and stop until the garage note), stop; own target never an obstacle, speed matched close behind it, kill-target ped exempt; the south-only against-the-flow allowance for chase; off-road snap after 100 or 300 ticks out of sight.
